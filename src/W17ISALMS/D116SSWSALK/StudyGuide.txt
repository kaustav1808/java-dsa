1. WHAT TO STUDY TODAY

   Prefix sums + monotonic deque of increasing prefix values (negatives break a plain
   sliding window). Daily loop (90 min): 15 min review Error Log and re-trace past
   failures; 45 min timed solve (talk through approach, trade-offs and edge cases before
   coding); 30 min review, optimise and log bugs.

   Key ideas to understand before coding (from today's problems):
   - Prefix sums with a monotonic increasing deque of indices: pop from the front while
     the sum condition holds, pop from the back to keep it increasing.
