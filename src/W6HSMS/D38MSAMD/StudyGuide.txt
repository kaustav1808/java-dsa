1. WHAT TO STUDY TODAY

   Stack basics, then a monotonic stack for next-greater problems, then a decreasing
   deque of indices for window maximums.

   Key ideas to understand before coding (from today's problems):
   - Push openers on a stack; on a closer, the top must be its partner.
   - Monotonic decreasing stack of indices: when a warmer day arrives, pop and fill in
     the waits.
   - Monotonic decreasing deque of indices: O(n) overall.
