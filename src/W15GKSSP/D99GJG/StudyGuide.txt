1. WHAT TO STUDY TODAY

   Track the farthest reachable index; for minimum jumps use the current window end.

   Key ideas to understand before coding (from today's problems):
   - Greedy: track the farthest index reachable; fail if you ever stand beyond it.
   - Greedy BFS by levels: track the farthest reach of the current jump window.
