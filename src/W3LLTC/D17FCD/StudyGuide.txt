1. WHAT TO STUDY TODAY

   Fast/slow pointers; locate the cycle entry by resetting one pointer to head and
   advancing both one step at a time.

   Key ideas to understand before coding (from today's problems):
   - Floyd's tortoise and hare: slow moves 1, fast moves 2; they meet iff there is a
     cycle.
   - After slow and fast meet, restart one pointer from head; moving both one step at a
     time, they meet at the cycle start.
