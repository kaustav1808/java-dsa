1. WHAT TO STUDY TODAY

   Use a dummy head to remove special cases when the head changes; merge and partition
   lists.

   Key ideas to understand before coding (from today's problems):
   - A dummy head node removes the special case for the first node.
   - Build two lists with two dummy heads, then join them; terminate the second list.
