1. WHAT TO STUDY TODAY

   Switch each pointer to the other list's head at the end so both travel A + B nodes.

   Key ideas to understand before coding (from today's problems):
   - Two pointers that switch to the other list's head at the end both travel lenA +
     lenB and meet at the intersection (or both reach null).
