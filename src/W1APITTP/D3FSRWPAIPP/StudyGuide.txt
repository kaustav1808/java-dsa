3. HOW TO WORK THROUGH THE DAY

   a. Concept first (30-45 min). Read about today's topic (section 1). Draw it on paper
      and work one tiny example by hand before touching code.
   b. For each problem, open its README.md. Restate the problem, list edge cases, trace an
      example by hand, and say the brute-force idea and its complexity before coding.
   c. Implement in the problem class, run Main with the README examples, then turn those
      examples plus your own edge cases into assertions in the Test class.
   d. Write the time and space complexity as a comment above your solution.
   e. Stuck for 20-25 minutes? Open the hint in the README, then close it and re-code
      the solution from scratch without looking.
   f. Log every mistake (wrong idea, off-by-one, missed edge case) in your Error Log with
      its root cause, not just the symptom.
