1. WHAT TO STUDY TODAY

   With positive numbers, if window [l..r] is valid then all r-l+1 subarrays ending at r
   are valid.

   Key ideas to understand before coding (from today's problems):
   - Sliding window: shrink while product >= k; each right end adds (right - left + 1)
     subarrays.
