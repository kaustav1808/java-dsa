   Running accumulator and int[26] frequency window; reuse the previous window instead
   of recomputing.

   Key ideas to understand before coding (from today's problems):
   - Fixed-size sliding window: add the new element, subtract the one leaving.
   - Fixed-size window of length p.length() with 26-slot counts; compare (or track a
     'matches' counter) as it slides.
