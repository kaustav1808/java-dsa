1. WHAT TO STUDY TODAY

   XOR properties, then a bitwise trie over 32 bits to pick the opposite bit greedily.

   Key ideas to understand before coding (from today's problems):
   - XOR all values: pairs cancel out (a ^ a = 0).
   - Binary trie of the numbers' bits (from bit 30 down); for each number walk the trie
     preferring the opposite bit.
