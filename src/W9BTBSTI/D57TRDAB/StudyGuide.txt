1. WHAT TO STUDY TODAY

   Top-down vs bottom-up recursion; return height and balanced flag in one pass.

   Key ideas to understand before coding (from today's problems):
   - depth = 1 + max(depth(left), depth(right)).
   - Post-order: return the height, or -1 as soon as any subtree is unbalanced (O(n)).
