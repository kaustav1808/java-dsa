1. WHAT TO STUDY TODAY

   dp[r][c] from top and left; initialise the edges; obstacles contribute 0.

   Key ideas to understand before coding (from today's problems):
   - dp[r][c] = dp[r-1][c] + dp[r][c-1]; a single row array is enough.
   - An obstacle cell contributes 0 paths; watch the start cell being blocked.
