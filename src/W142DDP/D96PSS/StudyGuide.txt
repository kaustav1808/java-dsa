1. WHAT TO STUDY TODAY

   Substring (contiguous): expand around centres. Subsequence (not necessarily
   contiguous): interval DP on [i..j].

   Key ideas to understand before coding (from today's problems):
   - Expand around each of the 2N-1 centres (odd and even lengths): O(N^2) time, O(1)
     space.
   - dp[i][j] = dp[i+1][j-1] + 2 if s[i] == s[j], else max(dp[i+1][j], dp[i][j-1]);
     equivalently LCS of s and reverse(s).
