1. WHAT TO STUDY TODAY

   Table dp[i][j]: match extends the diagonal, mismatch takes max of top/left.

   Key ideas to understand before coding (from today's problems):
   - dp[i][j] = dp[i-1][j-1] + 1 on a match, else max(dp[i-1][j], dp[i][j-1]).
