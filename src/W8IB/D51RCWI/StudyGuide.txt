1. WHAT TO STUDY TODAY

   Sort starts and ends, or use a min-heap of end times, to find peak concurrency.

   Key ideas to understand before coding (from today's problems):
   - Sort by start and use a min-heap of end times, or sweep sorted start and end arrays
     with two pointers.
   - Equals the maximum number of intervals overlapping at any point: sort by start with
     a min-heap of group end times, or a sweep line.
