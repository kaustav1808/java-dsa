1. WHAT TO STUDY TODAY

   Sort by start with a comparator on int[][] and sweep once, merging overlaps.

   Key ideas to understand before coding (from today's problems):
   - Sort by start, then extend or close the current interval.
   - Three phases: copy intervals ending before it, merge overlapping ones, copy the
     rest.
