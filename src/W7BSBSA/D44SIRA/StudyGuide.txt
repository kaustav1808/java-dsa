1. WHAT TO STUDY TODAY

   At least one half around mid is always sorted; decide which half contains the target.
   Handle duplicates (shrink bounds).

   Key ideas to understand before coding (from today's problems):
   - At each step one half is sorted; check whether the target lies in that half.
   - Like the distinct version, but when nums[lo] == nums[mid] == nums[hi] you can only
     shrink both ends by one (worst case O(n)).
